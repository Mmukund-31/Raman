## Curation rules (measured library)
A spectrum is accepted only if: the source confirms the identity (RRUFF "excellent", identity confirmed by XRD/chemistry; ROD `experimental`), provisional SNR ≥ 20, it covers at least 300–1100 cm⁻¹ with ≥ 300 points, the maximum is not flat-topped (saturation), and its material has ≥ 2 **independent** specimens. Independence means a different RRUFF sample id, or for ROD a different publication (papers often deposit several spectra of one sample). Mineral synonyms are merged (baryte → barite, celestite → celestine, systematic names of beryl/quartz/rutile/calcite), and RRUFF's unspecified "Tourmaline" is excluded because it overlaps the elbaite species. At most 3 specimens per material are kept, 532 nm first. Spectra are resampled to a 2 cm⁻¹ grid and min–max scaled; nothing else is changed. `curation_report.json` lists how many files were rejected and why.

## CubeRaman
[CubeRaman](https://github.com/jacobbusshart/CubeRaman) inspired this project. When checked on 2026-10-22 the repository page showed no LICENSE file, and its reference library is not published (only images of spectra). Therefore no CubeRaman code or data is reused.

## Curation rules (measured library)
A spectrum is accepted only if: the source confirms the identity (RRUFF "excellent", identity confirmed by XRD/chemistry; ROD `experimental`), provisional SNR ≥ 20, it covers at least 300–1100 cm⁻¹ with ≥ 300 points, the maximum is not flat-topped (saturation), and its material has ≥ 2 **independent** specimens. Independence means a different RRUFF sample id, or for ROD a different publication (papers often deposit several spectra of one sample). Mineral synonyms are merged (baryte → barite, celestite → celestine, systematic names of beryl/quartz/rutile/calcite), and RRUFF's unspecified "Tourmaline" is excluded because it overlaps the elbaite species. At most 3 specimens per material are kept, 532 nm first. Spectra are resampled to a 2 cm⁻¹ grid and min–max scaled; nothing else is changed. `curation_report.json` lists how many files were rejected and why.

## CubeRaman
[CubeRaman](https://github.com/jacobbusshart/CubeRaman) inspired this project. When checked on 2026-10-22 the repository page showed no LICENSE file, and its reference library is not published (only images of spectra). Therefore no CubeRaman code or data is reused.

## Diabetes urine study (`data/urine/`)
| Item | Category | Origin | License / terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| `data/urine/zenodo/` (250 spectra: 127 diabetic, 98 CKD without diabetes, 25 healthy volunteers) | **measured** | Senger R.S., Robertson J.L., Kavuru V., Choudhury D. (2022) *Raman spectra of urine from patients with diabetes mellitus and other pathologies*, [Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.7343559](https://zenodo.org/records/7343559). Agiltron PeakSeeker PRO-785, 785 nm. Converted by `backend/scripts/fetch_urine_zenodo.py`: median of the 10 replicate scans per sample, cropped to 400–1800 cm⁻¹, resampled to 2 cm⁻¹. The 33 "Surine" urinalysis-control samples are excluded. Source files were verified against Zenodo's MD5 checksums. | CC BY 1.0. Cite the dataset and Kavuru V. et al. (2023) PeerJ 11:e14879, doi:10.7717/peerj.14879 |
| `data/urine/trial/` (280 spectra, 70 participants × 4 visits) | **synthetic** | `data/urine/generate_urine_trial.py`: Lorentzian bands at approximate literature positions for urea, creatinine, uric acid, glucose, ketones and albumin, scaled by simulated concentrations, plus water, fluorescence and noise. Simulates a 12-week glycaemic-control monitoring trial; it describes no real study. | Generated by this project |
| `data/urine/examples/` (4 files) | **synthetic** | Same generator: normoglycaemic, glycosuria, glycosuria + ketonuria, and post-treatment urine | Generated by this project |

Caveat: in the measured dataset all DM+/DM− patients have chronic kidney disease, so group differences may reflect kidney disease, medication or collection conditions as well as diabetes. The cross-validated diabetic vs non-diabetic AUC on these data is about 0.60. Band assignments and methods also draw on Jeng et al. (2022) J. Clin. Med. 11(16):4829, Espinosa-Garavito et al. (2024) Sci. Rep., and Moreira et al. (2017) J. Photochem. Photobiol. B.
